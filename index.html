How it runs

From brief to ad ready file

Same pipeline every time, which is what keeps a weekly delivery schedule realistic.

00:01

Brief and product spec

You send the product, the angle and any reference ads. I lock the product spec before anything is generated so it stays identical across every scene in the campaign.

00:02

Script and hook

Hook written for the first three seconds, then the body of the script. You approve the words before a single frame is generated.

00:03

Generation, shot by shot

Each shot goes to whichever model handles it best: motion realism, character consistency, product fidelity or camera movement. No defaulting to one tool for everything.

00:04

Edit, grade and audio

Assembly, pacing, captions, colour and sound cut to the pacing of your reference. Weak shots get regenerated rather than hidden in the edit.

00:05

Delivery and variations

Final files in the aspect ratios you need, plus hook and angle variations so you have something to actually test against each other.

Tooling

Chosen per shot, not per habit

Every model is good at something and bad at something else. I pick per shot based on what the shot actually needs, then finish everything in one edit so the cut feels like one piece of film rather than a pile of clips.

The basics

What exactly is an AI UGC ad?

It is a user generated content style ad where the presenter, the scene, or both, are generated rather than filmed. It looks like a real person holding your product and talking to camera, but there is no casting call, no shoot day and no talent fee.

The format is the same one that performs on Meta and TikTok: a face, a hook, a demonstration, a reason to buy. Only the production route is different.

Do you use real actors or real footage?

The presenters are AI generated avatars. If you have real footage of your product, or real customer clips you want cut into the ad, I can build around those too. Many of the strongest ads mix both.

Will the ad look obviously AI generated?

That is the thing I spend the most time on. Shots that read as AI get regenerated rather than buried in the edit, and the finishing work, grade, pacing, audio and captions, is what closes most of the gap.

I will not promise a result no one can ever clock. I will tell you honestly which shots in a cut are the weakest before you run them.

What kinds of brands do you work with?

DTC and ecommerce brands running paid social: beverage, apparel, footwear, consumer electronics, skincare, supplements, accessories and similar physical products. If your product can be shown on camera, it can be made into this kind of ad.

My product is complex or technical. Does this still work?

Usually yes, though the approach shifts. Complex products lean more on product led visuals and demonstration than on an avatar talking. Send me the product and I will tell you before you pay whether this format suits it.

Scope, timing and price

How long does a video take?

A single UGC ad is typically a two to four day turnaround from approved script. A full product commercial takes longer because of the shot count and the grade. Weekly delivery cycles are something I already run for ongoing clients.

Rush work is possible. Tell me your deadline in the first message and I will say yes or no rather than making you wait.

How much does it cost?

My hourly rate starts at $17. For most projects a fixed price per deliverable is simpler, and I will quote that once I know the length, the number of variations and whether you need a full commercial or a single UGC cut.

Send the product and the brief and you will have a number back the same day.

What do you need from me to start?

Three things: clear product images or a link to the product page, the angle or claim you want the ad to lead with, and one or two reference ads whose style you like. Brand guidelines and any existing footage help but are not required.

How many variations do I get?

As many as your testing budget needs. Variations are usually the cheapest part of the job, because the script, the product spec and the edit structure already exist. Hook swaps, avatar swaps and aspect ratio versions are all straightforward once the first cut is approved.

How do revisions work?

Revisions are included and expected. You approve the script before generation starts, which removes most of the expensive rework. After the first cut, you send notes and I rework shots, pacing, captions or audio until it is right.

Can you work on a retainer or weekly schedule?

Yes. Ongoing weekly delivery is the setup that suits paid social best, because you need new creative before the current ad fatigues, not after. Tell me your volume and we can fix a cycle.

Output, formats and rights

What platforms are the ads built for?

Meta ads, TikTok and YouTube, plus Instagram Reels and Shorts. I deliver in the aspect ratios each placement needs, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5, with safe zones respected so captions and CTAs do not get cropped by the interface.

What files do I receive?

Final MP4s in every aspect ratio you asked for, captions burned in or supplied separately as SRT, and any variations. Project files and raw generations can be handed over on request.

Who owns the finished video?

You do. Once the work is paid for, the finished ads are yours to run, edit and repurpose across any platform, with no time limit or extra usage fee from me.

Can you match my brand's existing ad style?

Yes, and this is the part most people skip. I break a reference ad down into pacing, shot types, cut rhythm and hook structure, then rebuild that structure around your product. It is the difference between guessing at a style and actually matching it.

Will my product look accurate on screen?

The product spec gets locked before anything is generated, so the packaging, colour and proportions stay identical across every scene in the campaign. Product drift between shots is the most common failure in AI ads and it is avoidable.

Can you do voiceover in other languages or accents?

Yes. Voice is chosen or cloned to match the market you are selling into, and the script is tuned for read speed so the audio and the cut land together.

Do you only do short form, or longer pieces too?

Short form is the core of the work, but longer product commercials are part of it as well. The AETHER spot runs 38 seconds and full commercials can go longer where the story needs it.

Do you write the script, or do I?

I write it unless you would rather supply your own. Either way you approve the script before generation begins. If you have a script already, I will flag anything that will not survive the first three seconds.
